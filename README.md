Fact #2 — September 25, 2026
On September 25, 2026, Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic confirmed plans to establish the Standards Authority for Frontier AI (SAFA), a self-regulatory body modeled on FINRA. It will operate without government oversight.  The three companies approached former White House AI adviser Sriram Krishnan and former OSTP director Arati Prabhakar to lead it. Meta, xAI, and Nvidia are not participating. Source: The Information, Bloomberg, CNBC
